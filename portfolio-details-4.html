Project Overview

HealthFirst Clinics needed to dominate local search results across 12 locations. We implemented a comprehensive SEO strategy including technical optimization, content marketing, and local SEO that achieved page-1 rankings for 50+ competitive healthcare keywords.

The Challenge

The healthcare industry is highly competitive in search. HealthFirst had poor domain authority, thin content, and no structured data markup. Their competitors were outranking them for critical patient-acquisition keywords in every local market.

The Solution

We conducted in-depth keyword research, optimized all technical SEO factors (Core Web Vitals, schema markup, site architecture), created a medical content hub with 100+ expert-reviewed articles, and built high-authority backlinks through digital PR campaigns.
